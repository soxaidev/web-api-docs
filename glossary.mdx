SOXAI has two separate grouping systems. Their names can sound similar, but they serve different purposes.

```text
Company
└── Organization (Org)
    └── Member
        └── teams: a label only; it does not change access rights

Family Group
└── Members who choose which metrics to share with one another
```

## Company, organization, and member

A company can own one or more organizations. An organization owns a member roster. A member belongs to an organization and can have one or more `teams` labels. A `teams` label is not a permission boundary.

For the definitions and access rules that are enforced by the API, see [List organizations](/api-reference/organizations/list-organizations), [List an organization's members](/api-reference/organization-members/list-an-organizations-members), and the global **Who can read whose data** section in the API reference.

## Family Group

A Family Group is separate from a company or organization. Its members choose, per metric, what they share with one another. Family Group access is based on a member's role inside that group, not on an organization or company role.

See [Get one Family Group](/api-reference/family-groups/get-one-family-group) for the authoritative description and authorization rules. The adjacent Family Groups reference pages document the other operations.
